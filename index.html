Job description snapshot

Economic Development, Part-Time IT Specialist

Chiefs of Ontario is seeking an IT Specialist to support the First Nations Business Directory Portal and Economic Development data systems through Power BI, data quality, platform performance, secure information flows, portal implementation and technical support.

Role details

  • Organization: Chiefs of Ontario
  • Team: Economic Development
  • Location: 468 Queen Street East, Toronto, Ontario
  • Duration: ASAP to March 31, 2027
  • Base salary: $30,000 to $40,000 per year
  • Application status: accepted until filled

Core duties

  • Maintain Power BI models using DAX, Power Query and star schema design.
  • Support migration of the FNBD Portal into COO's internal IT environment.
  • Manage data ingestion, transformation, refresh pipelines and structured uploads.
  • Support Power BI Service workspaces, permissions, dashboards and refresh cycles.
  • Maintain validation processes, documentation, audit logging and knowledge transfer.
  • Support geocoding, portal information architecture and user interface improvements.

Important qualifications

  • 1 to 3 years in IT support, systems administration, data analytics or BI technical roles.
  • Power BI Desktop, DAX, data modelling and Power Query.
  • Power BI Service, workspaces, permissions and refresh cycles.
  • Survey or Excel dataset transformation.
  • ESRI ArcGIS visuals, geocoding tools or related geospatial exposure.
  • Data governance, privacy, cybersecurity and OCAP awareness.
